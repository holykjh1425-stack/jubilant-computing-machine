# SOLQ advertising, R&D institute and donation-compliance workplan

Date: 2026-11-02. Research and planning only. No entity registration, donation solicitation, tax filing, patent filing, spending or funding application executed.

## Advertising business
- Services: ad creative, landing pages, performance reporting, lead-intake automation, recurring campaign operations.
- Economics: setup fee + retainer + separately disclosed ad spend; distinguish client media spend from agency revenue.
- Compliance: check business registration, ad representations, platform terms, copyright, privacy consent, advertising labeling, contracts and cancellation terms.
- Pilot: produce a low-cost SOLQ campaign for owned channels first; measure qualified inquiries and conversions; no paid ads without authorization.
- Owner: MARKETING; FINANCE reviews margin; SECURITY_QA reviews data and compliance.

## R&D organization
- Technical themes: cost-controlled multi-agent orchestration, secure atomic customer intake, verifiable approval workflow, privacy-preserving automation, reusable vertical automation modules.
- Stage 1: R&D portfolio and novelty/prior-art research; record dated technical notebooks, test plans, results, contributors and expenses.
- Stage 2: Compare company-affiliated institute versus R&D dedicated department under current 2026 law; validate legal entity, full-time qualified personnel, physical space, equipment and documentation.
- Stage 3: Consult KOITA recognition process, tax professional on eligible R&D costs and deductions, patent counsel on novelty, claims, ownership, employer inventions and filing timing.
- Stage 4: Match actual eligible calls for grants/loans/guarantees; track applicant criteria, cofunding, reimbursement, audit, repayment and restrictions. No assumed awards.
- Research lab status and tax benefits must not be presented as automatic.
- Owner: BUSINESS/R&D; FINANCE for eligibility economics; SECURITY_QA for evidence; AI_AUTOMATION for prototypes.

## Church / nonprofit
- First validate genuine religious purpose, lawful registration/affiliation and authority to issue qualifying donation receipts.
- Distinguish individual donor tax credits from corporate deduction treatment; tax benefit belongs to eligible donor, not an automatic church revenue guarantee.
- Restricted donations must be used for designated religious/public-benefit purposes, documented and separately banked/accounted for.
- No commingling with SOLQ revenue, private benefit, sham donations, disguised advertising consideration or unapproved related-party transfers.
- Any arms-length commercial services must be independently contracted and reviewed for conflicts, fair value, tax and governance.
- Owner: NONPROFIT_COMPLIANCE role (planning only); FINANCE maintains a separate nonprofit ledger; legal/tax professionals review before action.

## Cross-business innovation
- R&D builds secure ad/CRM workflow components → SOLQ commercializes legitimate products → advertising agency tests lead generation → education trains users.
- Real estate/modular projects can become separate vertical markets for qualified automation/marketing services, with independent contracts and accounting.
- Nonprofit activities remain purpose-bound and must not be counted as owner net profit.

## Required scorecard
- Advertising: agency revenue vs pass-through ad budget, CAC, qualified leads, conversion, gross margin.
- R&D: actual qualified staff, documented projects, eligible expense evidence, IP novelty findings, grants applied/awarded/disbursed, repayment obligations.
- Nonprofit: eligible donations, donor receipts, restricted-fund balances, purpose expenditure, independent audits.
- Each pillar: owner, legal entity, stage, verified income, cost, cashflow, risk, next eligible task, evidence, approval.
