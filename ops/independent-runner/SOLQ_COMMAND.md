- ESTATE — property NOI, first-floor vacancy, Burger King or alternative tenant economics, commercial leases.
- MODU — modular housing dealership manufacturer contracts, logistics, warranty and margins.
- CARE — existing operating church, donation receipt eligibility verification, purpose-restricted funds and independent nonprofit accounting. Church donations are not SOLQ or owner profit.

## ATLAS mandatory direction to each role
1. Read latest WORK_LEDGER.md, tasks.json, COORDINATION_POLICY.md, WORKER_ROLES.md, DEDICATED_ASSIGNMENTS.md and this charter before any work.
2. Confirm the end goal and role scope. Select the next eligible task assigned to the role, check prerequisites, last completion evidence and existing run/lease.
3. Assign exactly one primary owner per task. Cross-role reviewers must not make concurrent writes.
4. Before autonomous writes, implement and validate atomic claims/lease recovery and audit trails. Without those, only manually coordinated planning is authorized.
5. Perform creative but falsifiable work: propose an opportunity, target customer, expected revenue, estimated cost, compliance gates, measurable experiment and stop-loss criterion. Do not fabricate metrics.
6. Protect existing 63 isolated PASS; do not repeat them, claim production validation, alter customer records, main or deployment.
7. Record task ID, owner, status, evidence URL/commit, side effects, cost, blocker, next action and approval needed.
8. Separate each legal entity's finances and the church's restricted nonprofit funds. FIN evaluates lawful tax optimization but no tax benefits are assumed automatically.
9. No new Work session, paid API call, financial charge, registration, contract, ad spend or production deployment without applicable authorization. Existing conditional Supabase Pro authorization remains subject to confirmed backup/restore feasibility and verified total cost, with extra approval above USD 25/month.
